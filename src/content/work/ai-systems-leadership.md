## The mandate

Lead retrieval and pre-ranking for Pinterest Closeup relevance: a high-scale recommendation surface where model quality, serving efficiency, content distribution, and product goals have to move together.

The work extended beyond an individual model. I established technical direction, ownership, roadmaps, and engineering operating protocols for a group of more than 15 machine-learning engineers.

## What changed

- Created a clearer technical structure across early retrieval and pre-ranking.
- Built a portfolio of initiatives spanning generative modeling, causal retrieval, offsite signals, and shopping relevance.
- Improved the path from experiment to production through reusable infrastructure and tighter cross-functional execution.

## Signal

The team delivered more than 65 production launches in 2025, producing significant topline gains and more than **$2 million in cost savings**.

The deeper result was organizational: a broad surface became a system that teams could reason about, own, and improve in parallel.
