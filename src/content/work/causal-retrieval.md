## Beyond correlation

Large-scale content distribution systems often optimize predictions built from historical interactions. That can reinforce exposure bias and spend computation where it creates limited incremental value.

I initiated a causal-inference approach to early-retrieval optimization, built the end-to-end MVP, and led productionization with cross-functional teams.

## From research to operations

The project joined a rigorous modeling question with practical systems constraints: how to estimate incremental value, integrate it into retrieval decisions, and validate the result at site scale.

## Signal

The production system delivered **$400K in cost savings**, **+0.2% sitewide sessions**, and **+1.3% repins**. The accompanying paper was accepted at KDD 2026.
