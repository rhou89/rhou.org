## Deep technology, applied

At Microsoft Research, I worked on quantum-inspired approaches to NP-hard combinatorial optimization and explored what they could deliver for practical industrial problems.

The work moved between scalable Python prototypes, Monte Carlo heuristics, machine-learning algorithms, benchmarking, and production-oriented C++ development.

## Translation

I designed and improved a fleet-management vertical solution on Azure Quantum for private preview. The resulting system delivered more than **15× acceleration** while also improving solution quality.

This project reinforced a principle that continues to shape my work: new computational ideas become valuable when they are tested against the full shape of a real problem.
